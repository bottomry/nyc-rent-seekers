SIGNIFICANT CHANGES FOR 2026
Welcome to the 2026 Edition of the NYCHA Development Data Book. The Performance Tracking and Analytics Department
(PTAD) has made various changes to the book this year to enhance its value as an information source.
Current Developments
This year, the Development Data Book includes data for all 219 developments in NYCHA’s portfolio as of January 2026.
New Updates
A new non-dwelling building (compactor room) was built in Morrisania Air Rights, adding to the NYCHA’s portfolio.
A collection facility is under construction at Polo Ground Towers, and it will be designated as a non-dwelling building.
