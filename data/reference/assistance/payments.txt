                      DSS CITYFHEPS PAYMENT STANDARDS
Note: The rent and utility amounts provided on this document are only valid for CityFHEPS
tenants who move within the five (5) boroughs of New York City. For tenants who move
outside of New York City, but within New York State, please go to the DSS CityFHEPS website
to find the statewide amounts at https://www1.nyc.gov/site/hra/help/cityfheps-documents.page.
The payment standard chart and payment standard guidelines for 2025 and 2026 are below.
The payment standard guidelines explain when the 2025 payment standards and 2025 utility
allowance amounts are applicable and when the 2026 payment standards and 2026 utility
allowance amounts are applicable.
                      Payment Standard Guidelines for 2025 and 2026
   •   All CityFHEPS Renewals and New CityFHEPS Packages Submitted Through
       February 28, 2026:
       All renewals and new CityFHEPS packages submitted until February 28, 2026, may use
       the 2025 payment standards and 2025 utility allowance amounts, unless the lease start
       date is April 1, 2026, or later.
   •   New CityFHEPS Packages Submitted on or After March 1, 2026:
       All CityFHEPS packages submitted on or after March 1, 2026, or with an April 1, 2026, lease
       start date, must use the 2026 payment standards and 2026 utility allowance amounts.
